This Cancellation and Refund Policy (“Policy”) forms part of the Terms and Conditions and applies to every order (“Order”) placed with Arya Vaidya Vilasini Vaidya Sala (“AVVVS”, “we” or “us”) through shop.avvvs.com. Capitalised terms not defined herein bear the meanings assigned to them in those Terms.

1. Refund or replacement in full

1.1 You shall be entitled, at your option, to a full refund or to a replacement where:

  1. a Product is delivered in a damaged or defective condition;
  2. the consignment is lost or damaged in transit;
  3. a Product is delivered after the expiry date stated on its label; or
  4. a Product comprised in the Order is not delivered, or a Product other than that ordered is delivered.

2. Procedure for claims

2.1 A claim under the preceding clause shall be made by email to mail@avvvs.com, stating the Order number in the subject line, or by telephone at +91 80892 02091.

2.2 A claim in respect of a damaged, defective or incorrect Product shall be accompanied by a photograph of the Product and its label.

2.3 A claim in respect of a missing Product shall identify the Product concerned, and shall be verified against our packing record for the consignment.

2.4 A claim shall be made as soon as reasonably practicable after delivery, and the Product and its packaging shall be retained until the claim is determined.

3. Return of unopened Products

3.1 A Product that is unopened, unused and in its original packaging may be returned, provided that notice of the return is given to us within thirty (30) working days of delivery.

3.2 A restocking charge of twenty per cent (20%) of the price paid shall be deducted from the refund.

3.3 Where the return is occasioned by a change of prescription by your physician, we may, upon verification of the revised prescription, waive the restocking charge and refund the price in full.

3.4 The cost of returning the Product shall be borne by you, and the refund shall be processed upon our receipt and inspection of the Product.

3.5 In the interests of patient safety, a Product that has been opened, and any Product identified on its page as non-returnable, shall not be accepted for return.

4. Cancellation

4.1 You may cancel an Order at any time before it is dispatched by notifying us, in which event the amount paid shall be refunded in full and no cancellation charge shall be levied.

4.2 An Order that has been dispatched may not be cancelled, and shall be dealt with under the clause headed “Return of unopened Products”.

5. Mode and time of refund

5.1 A refund shall be made to the original mode of payment. Upon approval of a refund, the amount shall ordinarily be credited within seven (7) to ten (10) working days, subject to the processing time of your bank or payment service provider.

5.2 Where payment was made in cash on delivery, the refund shall be made by bank transfer to an account nominated by you, and may take up to three (3) weeks from approval.

6. Grievances and statutory rights

6.1 Any grievance concerning a cancellation, return or refund may be raised in the manner set out on the Contact Us page.

6.2 Nothing in this Policy shall exclude or restrict any right or remedy available to you under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
