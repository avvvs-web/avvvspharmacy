3. Personal Data collected

3.1 We collect and process the following categories of Personal Data:

  1. Order and delivery data: your name, telephone number, email address, delivery and billing address, and the particulars of the products ordered, as provided by you at checkout;
  2. Payment data: the mode of payment, the amount paid and the status of the transaction. Card numbers, UPI PINs, net-banking credentials and other payment instrument details are submitted by you directly to the payment gateway (at present, Razorpay) from the checkout page hosted by Shopify, and are not received or stored by us;
  3. Account data: where you sign in, your name, email address, saved addresses, Order history and wishlist. Authentication is performed by Shopify by means of a one-time code sent to your email address, and no password is held by us;
  4. Prescription data: any prescription or physician’s note that you voluntarily attach to an Order, which constitutes Sensitive Personal Data;
  5. Stock alert data: your email address and the product in respect of which you request a notification of availability;
  6. Correspondence: the content of any communication you address to us by email, telephone or WhatsApp;
  7. Technical data: your internet protocol (IP) address, browser type and the pages requested, as recorded by our hosting provider, together with a count of requests originating from an IP address, retained for up to two (2) days for the prevention of abuse; and
  8. Analytics and advertising data: subject to your consent, information on pages viewed and products added to the cart, transmitted to Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel.

3.2 You are under no obligation to provide Sensitive Personal Data. The attachment of a prescription is optional, save where our pharmacist requires one before dispensing a particular product.

4.1 Personal Data is processed solely for the following purposes:

  1. to receive, verify, process, dispense, dispatch and deliver Orders, and to communicate with you in relation thereto;
  2. to enable our pharmacist to verify an Order against any prescription furnished;
  3. to create and administer your account;
  4. to respond to enquiries, complaints and grievances;
  5. to notify you of the availability of a product where you have so requested;
  6. to detect and prevent fraud, abuse and unlawful activity;
  7. to comply with obligations under Applicable Law, including the maintenance of tax, consumer and pharmacy records; and
  8. subject to your consent, to measure use of the Store and the performance of our advertisements, and to send you promotional communications.

4.2 We process Prescription data, Stock alert data, Analytics and advertising data and Personal Data used for promotional communications on the basis of your consent. All other Personal Data is processed because it is necessary for the performance of the contract of sale with you or for compliance with Applicable Law.

4.3 You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to mail@avvvs.com or, in respect of cookies, through the “Cookie settings” link at the foot of every page. Withdrawal shall not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before such withdrawal, and may result in our being unable to provide the service to which the consent relates.
